How to use

Find more of my projects Here

Starting: shift to Neutral or press the clutch fully, then tap Start.

Pulling away: pick 1st gear, rev to around a quarter to a third throttle, then ease the clutch up slowly.

Shifting: press the clutch most of the way down, drag the stick into the next gate, then release the clutch smoothly while easing on the gas.

Handbrake: slide it up to hold the car without your foot on the brake.

Uphill mode: gravity pulls the car backward on the slope. Rev to roughly 1500–2000 RPM, ease the clutch to the bite point, and roll onto the gas as you release the brake

Reverse: only selectable once the car is fully stopped.

All controls respond to multiple fingers at once.

Desktop not supported

This webapp is meant to be used with multiple gestures at the same time, the pedals, handbrake and gear stick are all drag controls meant for a phone screen. On a desktop with a mouse it may feel cramped or awkward to control precisely.

For a better experience, open this page on your phone.
